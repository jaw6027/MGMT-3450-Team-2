### About
SAIF Industries LLC is a family-owned supplier of surplus military and aviation parts, established in 1999 and based in Bakersfield, California. Its services include parts sourcing, inventory management, quality assurance, custom supply solutions, and global support.

Stocked, Sourced, Delivered.
For pricing requests, contact sales@saifind.com or visit saifindustries.com.
