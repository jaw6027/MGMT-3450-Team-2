## Scope and sources

Saif Industries LLC sources and supplies surplus military and aviation parts. Earlier conversation drafts describing it as a CNC machining or aerospace manufacturing business were superseded by the user-supplied company information and are not used in this marketing package.

The supplied text was attributed to https://www.saifindustries.com. Direct website and logo downloads returned proxy HTTP 403 errors in this environment, so the company's statements were not independently verified. The user subsequently attached the company's green circular logo in the chat.

Public business contact details are included as supplied. Username suggestions have not been checked for availability. The CAGE code is recorded as an identifier, not a certification or government endorsement. No specific certification, part airworthiness, guaranteed delivery date, or customer outcome has been established.

The generated green-and-white social graphic is visible in the conversation, but neither that graphic nor the uploaded logo was available as a local file when saving this package. Their absence is documented in the graphic brief; no substitute is presented as the original image. Upload the original files to add the exact assets to the repository.
